# Contributing to the Bullshit

The architectural discourse is an endless abyss of theoretical complexity, and I invite you to join me in expanding the **Architect Bullshit Generator (ABG)** archive. Your contributions help keep the academic inflation growing!

## How to Contribute

### 1. Suggesting New "Bullshit"
Have you encountered a particularly absurd piece of architectural jargon? Or perhaps a specific "Starchitect" whose language is a goldmine for parody?
* Please [open an issue](https://github.com/JC-datarchitect/architect-bullshit-generator/issues) and label it as "Suggestion." 
* Be as pretentious as possible in your description.

### 2. Proposing New Levels or Themes
If you have a vision for a new mode of discourse (e.g., "The Desperate Intern," "The Radical Urbanist," or "The High-Tech Prophet"), feel free to propose it.
* Open an issue to discuss the linguistic framework.

### 3. Submitting Code Improvements
If you want to refine the generation logic or add new features:
* Fork the repository.
* Work your magic in the code.
* Submit a Pull Request. Please ensure your additions are syntactically coherent—even if they are semantically empty.

## Code of Conduct
While the content is satirical, please keep the repository environment professional and respectful. Let’s focus the absurdity strictly on architectural discourse.
